Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Cookie settings

Flight Chalker (flightchalker.co.uk) is a darts scoring and league platform run by Adam Wheeler in the United Kingdom. I am the data controller for the personal information described below. This page explains what is collected, why, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and display name when you create a free account (including via Google sign-in, where we receive your name and email from Google). Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Darts data: match scores, statistics, game results, teams, fixtures and leagues you or your league organiser enter.
  • Team and league access: team PINs or codes used to unlock a team or league area. These are used only to control access.
  • Enquiries and emails: details you submit through enquiry or results-email features, and the email address results are sent to.
  • Usage data: anonymous visit statistics from Google Analytics (pages viewed, device type, approximate region), plus standard technical logs kept by our hosting provider.

Why we use it

  • To provide the scorer, training drills, stats, leaderboards and league tools you ask for (contract / legitimate interests).
  • To keep accounts and league data secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To send emails you request, such as match results, verification and password-reset messages.
  • To understand how the site is used so it can be improved (analytics, subject to your consent where required).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, it is under appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer arrangements.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember settings such as light/dark mode and unfinished match drafts. Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits, but only if you choose Accept on the cookie notice; it is not loaded otherwise. You can change your choice at any time with cookie settings. You can also block or clear these in your browser settings; essential features may stop working if you block storage used for sign-in.

How long we keep it

Account and match data are kept while your account or league is active. You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted at any time. Backups and security logs may persist for a short period afterwards.

How it is protected

Data is held in access-controlled databases, transmitted over HTTPS, and protected by row-level security so that team and league data is only available to the people entitled to it. Repeated incorrect PIN attempts lock access temporarily.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, object to or restrict how it is used, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise these rights, email privacy@flightchalker.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Flight Chalker is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. See also our terms of use.
